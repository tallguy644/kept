# Shift Ledger

A simple daily tracker for Lyft and Uber drivers. Log each day's earnings, rides and hours for each app, plus gas, miles, bonuses and deposits, and see what you actually kept.

**Open it:** https://tallguy644.github.io/shift-ledger/

- No account or sign-in needed. Works on phone and computer.
- Your entries are saved only in the browser on your own device. Nobody else can see them.
- Tap **Connect Google Drive** once. After that the app backs up daily and keeps a **Shift Ledger** Google Sheet in your Drive up to date (app to sheet only). Without connecting, use **Back up to Google Drive** now and then. On Android it opens the share menu: pick **Drive** to save the backup there. On a computer it downloads the backup file, which you can upload to drive.google.com.
- **Export to Excel / Sheets** saves a `.csv` you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.
- Use **Restore backup** with that `.json` file to restore your data on a new phone or computer.
- Tip: on your phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" so it opens like an app.
