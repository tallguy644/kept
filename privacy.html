Privacy Policy

Shift Ledger · Effective October 21, 2026

Shift Ledger is a daily tracker for rideshare drivers. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what it does with Google Drive if you choose to connect it.

What you enter

You can enter, for each day: date, earnings, number of rides and hours for Lyft and Uber, gas spending, miles driven, bonus or other income, and deposit type, date and amounts. The app does not ask for your name, phone number, location, or any account login for Lyft or Uber.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. The developer has no server and does not collect, receive, or see your data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from your device.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Connect Google Drive, you sign in with your own Google account and allow access using Google's most limited Drive permission (drive.file). With it, the app can only create and update one file it made itself, named Shift Ledger backup.json, in your Drive. It cannot see, open, or change any other files in your Drive.

The backup is sent directly from your device to your Google Drive. The developer never receives it. You can delete the file from Drive at any time, and you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Shift Ledger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Exports

When you use Export to Excel / Sheets or the backup button, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's save or share menu. Where it goes from there is your choice.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typefaces. Your device requests the font files from Google, which receives standard request information such as your IP address.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive are used only if you connect Drive, as described above.

Shift Ledger has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data.

Children

Shift Ledger is meant for adult drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact email listed on Shift Ledger's Google Play page.
