Kept: Rideshare Earnings Log

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max) — 78
Track Uber & Lyft earnings, miles and gas. See what you really keep each week.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Kept is the simple earnings tracker for rideshare drivers. Log your Uber and Lyft day in about a minute and see what you really kept after gas, miles and car costs. No GPS tracking draining your battery, no ads, and no bank login.

LOG YOUR DRIVING DAY IN A MINUTE
• One quick entry per day for Uber and Lyft: earnings, tips, rides and hours
• Add gas, miles driven, tolls, parking, repairs and bonuses
• A short tour shows you around the first time you open the app
• Edit or delete any day whenever you need to

SEE WHAT YOU REALLY KEEP
• Totals by day, week, month and year: what you earned, what you spent and what you kept
• Uber vs Lyft side by side: which app pays more per hour, per ride and per mile
• Earnings trends and simple insights compared with last week or last month

YOUR REAL COST PER MILE
• Enter your car once and Kept adds depreciation to your gas and repairs
• See what each mile really costs you, what you earn per mile, and your profit per mile
• Compare your real costs with the IRS mileage rate

MILEAGE LOG AND TAX-TIME TOTALS
• A yearly summary of income, business miles and the IRS standard mileage deduction
• Estimated self-employment tax and quarterly estimates with due dates
• A mileage log you can export or print for your records
(Estimates to help you file, not tax advice.)

FREE BACKUP AND SYNC
• Works without an account, and your days are saved on your phone
• Free Google Drive backup, with a Google Sheet of your days kept up to date
• Optional free account (email or Google) syncs your days to all your devices and keptforgigworkers.com
• Export to Excel or Google Sheets anytime
• Lock the app with your fingerprint or face
• No ads. Your data is never sold or shared.

KEPT PRO
Try Pro free for 7 days. Kept Pro adds the full Taxes report and the Compare and Trends tabs. Logging, totals, cost per mile, Google Drive backup and Excel export stay free. Monthly, yearly or one-time lifetime options.

MADE FOR GIG DRIVERS
Built for full-time and part-time rideshare drivers who want clear numbers without spreadsheets. Light and dark mode, and available in English, Español, Português, Français, Русский, 中文 and Tiếng Việt.

Kept is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Uber or Lyft.
