APP NAME (30 max)
Kept for Gig Workers

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Track Lyft & Uber earnings, gas, miles and what you really kept.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Kept is a simple daily tracker made for rideshare drivers. Log your Lyft and Uber day in under a minute, and see what you really earned after gas and costs.

LOG YOUR DAY, NOT EVERY TRIP
• One quick entry per day for Lyft and Uber: earnings, tips, rides and hours
• Add gas, miles, tolls and parking, and bonuses
• Edit or delete any day whenever you need to

SEE WHAT YOU REALLY KEPT
• Totals by day, week, month and year
• Lyft vs Uber side by side: which app pays more per hour, per ride and per mile
• Earnings trends and simple insights, compared with last week or last month

TAX-TIME TOTALS
• A yearly summary of your income, business miles and IRS standard mileage deduction
• Estimated self-employment tax and quarterly estimates with due dates
• A mileage log you can export or print for your records
(Estimates to help you file, not tax advice.)

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Works without an account, and your days are saved on your phone
• Optional free account (email or Google) keeps your days backed up and synced on all your devices and at keptforgigworkers.com
• Lock the app with your fingerprint or face
• Save a backup file anytime and restore it on any device
• No ads. Your data is never sold or shared.

KEPT PRO
Try everything free for 30 days. Kept Pro unlocks the Taxes report, automatic Google Drive backup with a Google Sheet, and Excel export. Monthly, yearly or one-time lifetime options.

Available in English, Español, Português, Français, Русский, 中文 and Tiếng Việt.
