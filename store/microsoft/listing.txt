DESCRIPTION
Kept is the simple earnings tracker for rideshare drivers. Log your Uber and Lyft day in about a minute and see what you really kept after gas, miles and car costs. It works on your PC, your phone and at keptforgigworkers.com, with your days synced between them.

LOG YOUR DRIVING DAY IN A MINUTE
• One quick entry per day for Uber and Lyft: earnings, tips, rides and hours
• Add gas, miles driven, tolls, parking, repairs and bonuses
• A short tour shows you around the first time you open the app

SEE WHAT YOU REALLY KEEP
• Totals by day, week, month and year: what you earned, spent and kept
• Uber vs Lyft side by side: which app pays more per hour, per ride and per mile
• Earnings trends compared with last week or last month

MILEAGE LOG AND TAX-TIME TOTALS
• A yearly summary of income, business miles and the IRS standard mileage deduction
• Estimated self-employment tax and quarterly estimates with due dates
• A mileage log you can export or print
(Estimates to help you file, not tax advice.)

FREE BACKUP AND SYNC
• Free Google Drive backup with a Google Sheet of your days
• Optional free account syncs your days across your PC, phone and the web
• Export to Excel anytime
• No ads. Your data is never sold or shared.
